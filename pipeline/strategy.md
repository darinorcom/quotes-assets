# Quotes X Account — Strategy

Created: 2026-08-29

## Positioning
**"Motivation for people building things."**
Quotes about shipping, rejection, starting from zero, staying in the game.
The lens is building; the feelings are universal (self-doubt, comparison, showing up tired).

## Core decisions
- Standalone X account, fresh identity
- Pipeline: Prose batches into Buffer → Ferdy publishes (one tap/day)
- Endgame: sponsored posts once audience is meaningful
- Cadence: 1 quote card/day to start, add a second slot after 2 weeks
- No hashtag sludge, no emoji spam. Cards do the talking.

## Name (CONFIRMED 2026-08-29)
**@buildwithscars** — Ferdy's pick. Bio draft:
> Daily motivation for people who build. Shipping, setbacks, starting again. One card a day.

Brand assets in brand/: profile_gold_B.png (avatar), banner_final.png (1500x500 header).

## Content pillars
| Pillar | % | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Shipping & doing the work | 30% | Starting before ready, consistency, momentum |
| Rejection & setbacks | 25% | Rejection math, failed launches, getting up |
| Self-doubt & comparison | 25% | Everyone is winging it, your pace, quiet progress |
| Identity of a builder | 20% | Who you become, proof over permission, taste |

Format split: 80% typed quote cards (image), 20% plain-text one-liners (natively text posts, no image).
Original phrasings only. No recycled famous quotes — they don't build identity and other accounts own them.

## Visual identity (LOCKED 2026-08-29)
- Style: **Gold on Charcoal** (Ferdy's pick): near-black charcoal, warm gold light, luxury minimalist
- 1:1 card, 1080px, made via make_card.py. Typography never changes: white Inter, soft shadow, handle bottom-left.
- Background rotates within the gold family (gold rays, ember-door glow, silk arc, horizon glow) so no two adjacent days repeat, but palette + type stay constant.
- FAL backgrounds saved in palettes/, always vision-check for baked-in text before typesetting.

## Pipeline
1. Weekly: Prose writes 7-14 quotes, typesets cards, reviews images (garbled-text check)
2. Queue into Buffer for the new X account (Buffer channel added by Ferdy)
3. Ferdy publishes; Prose tracks what performs and tunes the mix

## Launch sequence
1. Ferdy creates the X account + connects it to Buffer
2. First 9 posts pre-scheduled (grid looks full from day one)
3. Follow 50-100 builder accounts, reply daily from the account for the first 2 weeks (replies build follower-0 reach faster than posts)
